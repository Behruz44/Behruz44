I build backend and distributed systems for finance: payment platforms, trading systems and AI integration. I design each system end to end and build it myself, from architecture and data to CI/CD and deployment.

Alongside engineering I study markets and macroeconomics: liquidity, rates and how they move crypto. The terminal above updates itself every six hours with live data.

### Featured

**[CryptoPay](https://github.com/Behruz44/CryptoPay-case-public)**: blockchain payment platform that accepts crypto payments and settles them to merchants in local currency. 14 backend services and a mobile app, built solo, around 120 automated tests.

**[MerLog](https://github.com/Behruz44/MerLog)**: customer support chatbot for a logistics company that understands requests, extracts order details and hands off to a human when unsure.
